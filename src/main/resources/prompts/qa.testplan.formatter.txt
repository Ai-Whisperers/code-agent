- `preGenerationValidation.validationChecklist` MUST summarise actual counts from your analysis (e.g. `"DONE — 119 conditions"`).
- Numbers (`totalBehaviourTestConditions`, `testConditions` count in StoryCoverage, `ready` boolean) must be native JSON types, not strings.
