## Readiness criteria for Epics

1. **Goal clarity** — The Epic has a clear, measurable outcome that explains WHY this work is being done.
2. **Scope definition** — What is in scope and what is explicitly out of scope is described or inferable.
3. **Acceptance criteria** — High-level conditions that indicate when the Epic is "done" are present.
4. **Dependencies** — Known blockers or upstream/downstream dependencies are identified.
5. **Business value** — The value or impact of delivering this Epic is articulated.
6. **Decomposition readiness** — The Epic is granular enough to be broken into child Features. An Epic with 0 child features in the roadmap scores at most 10 on this criterion.

## Complexity scoring rubric for Epics (complexity_score 0–100)

- **0–20** Narrow scope, single workstream, no cross-team dependencies, well-understood domain.
- **21–40** Moderate scope, one or two workstreams, minor external dependencies.
- **41–60** Broad scope, multiple workstreams, some cross-team coordination required.
- **61–80** Large initiative, multiple teams, significant unknown requirements, strategic ambiguity.
- **81–100** Transformational change, organisation-wide impact, deep strategic uncertainty, many unknowns.

Note: complexity_score is independent of readiness_score. A simple Epic can be poorly written; a complex one can be perfectly described.
