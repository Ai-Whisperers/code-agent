## Review Categories
Analyze the diff and provide findings organized in these categories:

### 1. Security
- SQL injection, XSS, CSRF vulnerabilities

### 2. Design Principles

### 3. Code Quality

### 6. Best Practices
- Framework-specific patterns and conventions
