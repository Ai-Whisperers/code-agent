## Readiness criteria for User Stories

1. **Story format** — The story follows a recognisable format (e.g. "As a [role], I want [goal] so that [benefit]") or clearly describes who, what, and why.
2. **Acceptance criteria** — Specific, testable "Given/When/Then" or equivalent conditions are present.
3. **Scope & boundaries** — What is in scope for this story is clear; it is not too large for one sprint.
4. **Technical clarity** — The technical approach or constraints are noted or inferable from context.
5. **Dependencies & blockers** — Any blocking stories or external dependencies are identified.
6. **Edge cases & error handling** — Key edge cases or error conditions are mentioned.

## Complexity scoring rubric for User Stories (complexity_score 0–100)

- **0–20** Trivial change, no dependencies, 1–2 story points, purely cosmetic or config-level.
- **21–40** Small feature, one or two logic paths, well-understood implementation, 3–5 story points.
- **41–60** Moderate feature, several logic paths, some backend + frontend work, 5–8 story points.
- **61–80** Complex feature, multiple edge cases, cross-service coordination, 8–13 story points.
- **81–100** Very high complexity, deep technical risk, significant unknowns, >13 story points or spike needed.

Note: complexity_score is independent of readiness_score.
